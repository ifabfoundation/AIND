What is AR/MA order?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AR(p): Autoregressive model of order p (depends on p previous values)
MA(q): Moving average model of order q (depends on q previous errors)

CRITICAL: If AR/MA orders differ, the synthetic model may be:
  - Overfitting (too high order)
  - Underfitting (too low order)
  - Using wrong temporal dependencies
